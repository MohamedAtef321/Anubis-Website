The two minds

A throne above,
a thousand hands below

Most agents cram planning and execution into one overloaded brain — slow, costly, fragile. ANUBIS splits the soul in two: the Master reasons, delegates and judges; the slaves grind through browsing, writing, coding and filing in parallel.

  • Strategic tokens spent only where thought matters
  • Cheap execution models do the heavy lifting
  • Failures are contained, retried and re-decreed
The Master Mind
A frontier-class reasoning model. It perceives your intent, decomposes it into decrees, allocates resources, weighs results against the feather of truth, and composes the final verdict. It never touches raw labor.
The Slave Legion
Sacred orders — Researcher, Scribe, Navigator, Artisan, Steward, Herald — each a focused executor tuned for speed and cost. Spawned per task, dismissed when done.
The Golden Scales
A judgment layer between plan and action: risk scoring, budget checks, approval gates. Nothing executes until it balances.
The Living Record
Every perception, decree and tribute is inscribed in an auditable ledger — replayable step by step, exportable as evidence.
